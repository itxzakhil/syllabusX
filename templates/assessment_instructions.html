Assessment Instructions

Please read the following instructions carefully before starting your assessment.

Assessment Structure

1

Course Selection

Select the course for which you want to take the assessment.

2

MCQ Assessment

You will receive 20 multiple-choice questions. The questions will be shuffled for each student.

3

MCQ Time Limit

You will have 20 minutes to complete the MCQ section.

4

Coding Assessment

After completing the MCQ section, you will move to the coding section.

5

Coding Questions

The coding section will contain 3 programming questions.

6

Coding Time Limit

You will have a total of 60 minutes for the coding section.

⚠ Important

  • Make sure you have a stable internet connection.
  • Do not refresh the browser during the assessment.
  • Do not close the browser window while taking the assessment.
  • Read each question carefully before answering.
  • Your assessment activity will be recorded.
  • Your results will be saved in the college assessment system.
